FAQ

Everything you might want to know about joining the team.

How can I join?

Fill out our application by clicking Join Us at the top of the page! We recruit at the start of every semester.

Do you require any experience to join the club?

No background experience is required to join the club, but we welcome any previous experience in 3D printing.

Do you have to be an engineering student?

Not at all! We have students from all different majors, ranging from movement science to neuroscience.

Do you have to be an undergraduate student to join?

We welcome graduate students and even those who aren't in university! Just get in contact with us and we'll see how we can get you involved.

When do you accept applications and when are results out?

We recruit students every semester during the first 3 weeks, and results are released near the latter half of that time frame.

How long is training for subteams?

Training duration varies depending on which subteam you're accepted into, and can range from 4 to 10 hours.

What is the weekly commitment?

The weekly time commitment varies depending on the subteam and can range from 1 to 3 hours per week.

Are students limited to joining only one subteam?

Students are able to join multiple subteams, and many explore different teams across semesters.

What are you looking for in an applicant?

People who are self-motivated, creative, remain committed, and communicate well with the club!

Still have a question? Email us and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
