Why?

The need is real, and the existing options fail growing kids.

1 in 2,540

children in the United States are born with upper limb reductions.

6,000+

children in the US receive upper limb amputations each year.

For these children, routine activities like cooking and getting dressed become significantly more challenging. Prosthetics can restore independence, but the reality of the market makes them nearly unattainable for many families.

A standard myoelectric prosthetic arm averages $20,000, and because children grow, it needs to be replaced every 12–18 months. Existing options also offer limited customization and demand high maintenance.
