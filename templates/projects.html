Michael's Single Motor Arm

IN PROGRESS

Michael, 18, was born with an underdeveloped left arm below the elbow. Growing up, that meant questions from classmates, extra effort on monkey bars and push-ups, and everyday hurdles with two-handed tasks like cooking and yardwork.

His family's first prosthetic was uncomfortable and limited. Working with our team, he now has a fully customized, Terminator-themed robotic arm with lights — and voice control coming next.

“All the students make me feel special and important. Never give up. Don't ever let your disability stop you.”

— Michael

Julian's Arm

COMPLETED

We first met Julian when he was eight years old, through the E-Nable organization. His electric arm had become outgrown and a replacement was unaffordable, so our team designed and built a body-powered prosthetic arm for him.

Body-powered prosthetics are a much cheaper alternative that provide essential functions through specific body movements, a practical, durable option for a fast-growing kid. Julian has since progressed from his mechanical arm to a myoelectric arm.
