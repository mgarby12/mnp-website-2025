Our Team

We are made up of six subteams, each with a unique role that contributes to the development of our final product.

MECHANICAL

CAD design, 3D printing, assembly, stability/quality testing

ELECTRICAL

Circuit board production and embedded electronics

INTERFACES

Electrode garments, conductive threads, advanced features

OUTREACH

Community engagement, conferences, recipient coordination, fundraising

SOFTWARE

Microcontroller algorithms, signal processing, website/app design

ASSEMBLY

Assembly of components, physical testing, quality assurance
